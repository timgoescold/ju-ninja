privacy & recording terms

Version 2026-11-26-v1

what gets collected

You can browse and download without creating a community account. When you contribute, Supabase creates an anonymous account and stores a session in your browser. We store your account identifier, contributions, timestamps and, for voice submissions, your consent version and time. Hosting, security and download providers may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs. We use GitHub Pages, Cloudflare (including Turnstile and R2), Supabase and jsDelivr to operate the site. These services may process data outside your country.

what we do with it

We use contributions to develop the game, show poll totals and community statistics, moderate submissions, prevent abuse and operate the site. Public answers may appear after review; private answers are not published by the site. Do not submit personal or sensitive information. No additional advertising or visitor analytics are installed by us.

recording terms

Voice submissions are for people aged 18 or older. By checking the consent box and submitting, you confirm that you made the recording, have the rights to its contents and have not included other people's voices or copyrighted material without permission. You retain ownership and grant the Ju-Ninja developer a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to edit, reproduce and distribute the recording as part of Ju-Ninja and its promotion. No payment or inclusion is promised. Your upload is private, but selected audio may become public in the game or promotional material. You may request that we stop future use; copies already distributed may not be retrievable.

how long we keep things

We aim to remove unfinished recordings after seven days and rejected recordings within 30 days of rejection. Cleanup is manual. Other contributions and selected recordings may be retained while useful for the game or its operation. Service logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of your information or withdrawal of recording permission by contacting the developer at tolsen056@gmail.com. We may need information to verify your request. Clearing browser storage may remove your ability to prove ownership of an anonymous account. Applicable privacy rights are not limited by these terms.

Community participation is optional. If you are under 13, please do not submit community contributions.
